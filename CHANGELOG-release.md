## 1.62.4
**Player Castbar:**
- Everything about the cast timer now sits in a section of its own, Cast timer, instead of partly in General and partly behind a gear where it was not found.

**Priest:**
- On Season of Discovery the Vampiric Touch row of the DoT tracker stayed empty behind a placeholder icon and the mana counter never counted: the tracker only knew the talent, not the rune. It knows both now, and the counter uses the rune's own 2 percent there.

**Settings Window:**
- Switches in the right-hand column of a two-column page now line up with the switches on full-width rows; they stood further right before.

**Unit Frames:**
- A target of target that the game had pinned to a fixed spot on the screen stayed there while the target frame moved. The addon now drops that pin out of combat, and after the next reload the frame hangs at the target frame again.
